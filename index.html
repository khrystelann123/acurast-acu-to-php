COMMUNITY WITHDRAWAL GUIDE

From ACU
to PHP

Turn your Acurast rewards into pesos — step by step.

Received some ACU for providing compute on Acurast and want it in your GCash, Maya or bank account? Follow the four-step route below.

ACU→Gate→USDT→PHP→GCash · Maya · Bank

BEFORE YOU START

What you need

Acurast Hub account with ACU

Where your rewards land. Sign in at hub.acurast.com and check your ACU balance.

A Gate account

The exchange you'll route through. It needs to be verified and ready to receive a deposit.

A PHP payment method

GCash, Maya, or a bank account — wherever you want the pesos to arrive.

READ THIS BEFORE STEP 4

In P2P trading, never confirm or release the transaction until you have opened GCash, Maya or your bank app and seen the money arrive. A buyer's screenshot is not proof.

THE ROUTE, END TO END

4 simple steps

1

STEP ONE

Deposit ACU to Gate

First, get your ACU deposit address from Gate. You're not sending anything yet — you're just fetching the address your ACU will be sent to.

  1. Open the Gate app.
  2. Tap Assets → Deposit → Onchain Deposit.
  3. Search for ACU and select the result labelled Acurast.
  4. Select the Acurast network and copy the ACU deposit address.
Important: The deposit network on Gate must match the network you send from. Always copy and paste the address and check the first and last characters.

2

STEP TWO

Transfer ACU from the Acurast Hub to Gate

Now send the ACU from the Hub to the Gate address you just copied.

  1. Open hub.acurast.com and go to your ACU balance.
  2. Select Transfer.
  3. Paste the Gate ACU deposit address, enter the amount, and confirm.
Keep some ACU for gas. Don't transfer your full balance. Leave a little behind for transaction fees.
Show visual guide
3

STEP THREE

Trade ACU for USDT

ACU doesn't sell straight into pesos, so first swap it for USDT — the stablecoin that PHP buyers trade against.

  1. In Gate, open Trade → Spot Trading.
  2. Search for the ACU/USDT pair.
  3. Select Sell, enter the amount of ACU, and confirm the order.
Tip: A Limit order only fills at the price you set. For an immediate sale, the guide notes that you can switch to Market and take the current price.
Show visual guide
4

STEP FOUR

Sell USDT for PHP

The last hop: Gate → P2P → Sell USDT → PHP, then choose your preferred payment method.

  1. Navigate to P2P.
  2. Pick Sell and choose USDT.
  3. Select PHP and your payment method.
  4. Choose an available buyer and review their price, trade count and completion rate.
  5. Enter the amount and place the order.
  6. Wait for the buyer to pay.
  7. Open your GCash, Maya or bank app and verify the money arrived.
  8. Only then confirm and complete the transaction in Gate.
The one rule that matters: If the money never arrives, don't confirm. Raise a dispute with Gate support instead.
